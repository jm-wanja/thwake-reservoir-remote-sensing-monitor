# 0004 — Phased scope, ongoing cadence

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-10-09
- **Deciders:** Julie Mugira

## Context
The owner wants to answer all questions (filling, water quality, regional change) for a mixed audience, as an ongoing project. Doing everything at once risks never shipping.

## Decision
Deliver in phases: **1 Baseline → 2 Filling tracker → 3 Water quality → 4 Regional change**, then update monthly (automated) with human reviews after each rainy season. Each phase has exit criteria ([05-roadmap.md](../docs/05-roadmap.md)).

## Alternatives considered
- **Filling tracker only** — achievable but loses the distinctive water-quality angle.
- **Everything in parallel** — high risk of stalling.

## Consequences
- A public link exists early (Phase 2) and grows.
- Phase 1 must happen before/at the start of impoundment to be clean.
